The Person Behind the Work

About Me

My name is Stephen Gutierrez, and I am currently a student at Loyola University Chicago. I am Mexican American, born and raised in Texas, with a passion for all things mystical and artistic.

I've been playing guitar since I was 12 years old, and I love uploading covers to my TikTok. I also have a passion for the stars, having spent the past five years studying astrology rooted in history and tradition.

I served four years in the United States Marine Corps, stationed at Camp Pendleton, California.

As a psychology major, I hope to one day bring together my different interests and experiences into my therapeutic practice. My goal is to eventually open my own practice, incorporating multiple avenues of healing and self-expression to help others.

My Astrology Work

Astrology has been a major part of my life for the past five years. My studies focus on historical and traditional approaches to understanding the stars.

Through Hermes Homie, I explore Islamicate, ancient Babylonian, and Hellenistic astrological traditions.

I enjoy studying the history behind these practices and sharing my knowledge with others who are curious about astrology.

Beyond the Stars

My Life Through Music

I've been singing and playing guitar since I was 12 years old. Music has always been much more than a hobby for me. It's one of my greatest passions.

I have a particular love for jazz, indie, and shoegaze. Each genre gives me a different space to experiment with sound, emotion, and creativity.

Playing guitar and singing allow me to explore and express all aspects of my personality. Music is a way for me to connect with myself and communicate feelings that words cannot always capture.

I also enjoy recording and sharing guitar covers on TikTok.
