About Me

My name is Stephen Gutierrez, and I am currently a student at Loyola University Chicago. I am Mexican American, born and raised in Texas, with a passion for all things mystical and artistic.

I've been playing guitar since I was 12 years old, and I love uploading covers to my TikTok.

I also have a passion for the stars, having spent the past five years studying astrology rooted in history and tradition.

I served four years in the United States Marine Corps, stationed at Camp Pendleton, California.

As a psychology major, I hope to one day bring together my different interests and experiences into my therapeutic practice.

My goal is to eventually open my own practice, incorporating multiple avenues of healing and self-expression to help others.

My Astrology Work

Astrology has been an important part of my life for five years.

I study historical and traditional approaches to astrology, including ancient Babylonian, Hellenistic, and Islamicate traditions.

Through Hermes Homie, I explore the history of astrology and share my knowledge with others.

My Life Through Music

I've been singing and playing guitar since I was 12 years old.

Music is much more than a hobby. It's one of my greatest passions and a way to express the different aspects of my personality.

I have a particular love for jazz, indie, and shoegaze. These genres allow me to explore different sounds, emotions, and creative possibilities.

Music gives me the freedom to explore myself and express feelings that words cannot always capture.

I also love recording and uploading guitar covers to TikTok.
